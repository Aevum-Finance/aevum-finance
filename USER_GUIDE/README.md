# Aevum User Guide

Welcome to Aevum — budgeting that turns everyday spending into future savings.
This guide explains what Aevum does and how to use it, in plain language. No
code, no setup; if you're here to *build* Aevum, head to
[CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md) instead.

## Why "Aevum"?

*Aevum* is Latin for an age — an enduring duration; time that accrues rather than
runs out. Written another way, *एवं* is Sanskrit for *and* — *together with*.
Between them they hold the whole idea: money is the most patient thing you own,
and Aevum is a tool for everyone, not just people who already speak the language
of finance.

Most money apps are built on fear — they're good at making you anxious and not so
good at making you wealthier. Aevum starts from the opposite premise: time isn't
running out, it's accruing, and so is your money — gently, one ordinary day at a
time. No pressure, no shame, no discipline required — just a calmer way to turn
today's spending into tomorrow's security.

## The one-minute version

You record what you spend. Aevum sorts each expense into a category and sets
aside a small **self-imposed tax** — a fraction of the amount — into your
**savings account**, building a provision for that kind of expense in the
future. You also set **budgets**; overspend one and Aevum adds a **penalty** on
top. At the end of each week it all becomes a single **bill** you settle by
moving the money into your savings account. Over time, everyday spending quietly
builds a real savings balance.
